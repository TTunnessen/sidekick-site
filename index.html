Sidekick

Sidekick is a personal desktop app for planning: goals, a daily planner, a calendar view, and an inbox that sorts your email by what needs doing.

It is a private, single-user app. It runs on your own computer and keeps its data there. It connects to Google (Gmail and Google Calendar) only with your permission, so it can show your events and email and, when you approve a message, send it for you.

Sidekick is not offered to the public and is not for sale.

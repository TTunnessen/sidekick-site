Sidekick Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Sidekick is a personal desktop app used by its developer. This policy explains what it does with your information, including data from Google accounts you connect.

What Sidekick accesses

  • Gmail: to read your messages so they can be shown and sorted in Sidekick's inbox, and to send emails that you have reviewed and approved one by one.
  • Google Calendar: to read your events so they can be shown in Sidekick and used to plan your day, and to create events when you ask it to.
  • Your Google account email address: to label which account is connected.

How it is used

Google data is used only to provide these features to you inside the app. Sidekick never sends an email without your approval of that specific message.

Where it is stored

Sidekick stores your data on your own computer. Sign-in tokens are kept in your operating system's secure storage. Emails and events are processed on your own devices, including by an AI model that runs locally. Sidekick has no servers of its own, and your Google data is not uploaded to any developer-run service.

Sharing

Sidekick does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. It does not use Google data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Sidekick's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can disconnect an account in Sidekick's Settings at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder on your computer deletes everything Sidekick stored.
